Why We Do This

PalFinder exists because too many animals end up without a home, and too many people looking to adopt don't know where to start. We built this site to act as a central, searchable home for registered animals that don't yet have owners, pulling every listing straight from our database so information stays accurate and up to date.

We also believe adoption works best as a match, not a guessing game. That's why we're building a matching system: tell us the traits you're looking for in a pet, and we'll compare your preferences against the tags attached to every animal in our database to help you find the companion who actually fits your life.
